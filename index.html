My Designs
of Products

I love taking a single design idea and translating it across different materials — from canvas bags to ceramic mugs, stickers to packaging. Every project starts with a concept, then finds its own logic in color, scale and texture depending on where it lands. The piece shown here — a tote bag built around Renaissance-inspired artwork — is one example of that process: classical imagery reworked into something wearable and everyday.

BEHIND
THE PROCESS

Every piece starts long before the final render — with sketches, color tests and a lot of trial and error. This is a closer look at how an idea actually gets built: the references I collect, the drafts that don't make it, and the small decisions that shape the final piece. It's less polished than the finished work, but it's where the real thinking happens.
